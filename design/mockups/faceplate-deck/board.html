what changes

  1. One object, one metaphor. The phone becomes the player: a cassette seen through a smoked door, keys at the bottom.
  2. Horizontal cassette. The label reads normally with handwritten title, side letter, real C-90 timing, and an album-art sticker.
  3. Mechanical counter replaces the clock. RESET zeroes it like the real thing.
  4. Real L/R meter. Proper scale (−20…+3), peak hold, clip LED. Must be fed by actual PCM levels.P0
  5. Honest switches. TAPE EQ and DOLBY NR are visible slide switches defaulting to FLAT / OFF. The green DIRECT lamp shows when no processing is applied.P0
  6. Piano keys with travel. 62×70dp targets (well above 48dp). PLAY latches down. Dark glyph on tangerine passes contrast (4.7:1).P0
  7. STOP and EJECT are separate. EJECT still opens the library, so navigation is unchanged.
  8. Restrained glass glare and a single tangerine accent. Emerald only for status lamps (RUN, DIRECT), per the brand guide.
