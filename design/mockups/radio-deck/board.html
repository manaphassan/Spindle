what changes

  1. Same machine, tuner section. Chassis, header band, switch and key geometry match the tape deck. Swiping to radio should feel like the same device, not a different app.
  2. Slide-rule dial. A linear 87.5–108 MHz scale with the four carrier stations marked by callsign. The tangerine needle and active-station marker are the only accents in the window. Drag the dial to tune.
  3. The LCD shows real state. Big frequency on ghost segments, preset slot, STEREO/MONO indicator, and the RDS line, all fed by onSignalChanged.P0
  4. Thumbwheel tuning. 0.1 MHz detents with a haptic tick on each step. SEEK buttons jump between carriers (existing skip behaviour).
  5. SIG + centre-zero TUNE meter. Driven by getSignalStrength and getCarrierDeviation. When the centre pair lights, the station is locked. No fake animation.P0
  6. Visible switches. BAND FM/NET replaces the mode toggle. MODE AUTO/MONO maps to isForceMono. The emerald STEREO lamp lights only on a real pilot.P0
  7. Preset piano keys. 4 latching 62×70dp keys plus SCAN, the same targets as the transport. Tap tunes, hold stores (unchanged behaviour).
  8. NET keeps the layout. Streams sit at their nominal frequency on the dial. FM-only controls dim instead of disappearing, so nothing jumps.
  9. No antenna, no FM. When the headset is unplugged, the LCD shows NO ANT and the needle parks (see d).

c · scanning

Silver, mid-sweep at 95.7. SCAN latched, presets released, needle trails as it climbs.

d · no antenna

Pro black, headset unplugged. FM controls dim, the needle parks grey, and the LCD says why.

states

  1. SCAN latches like a key. While sweeping, SCAN stays pressed in tangerine and the presets release. Tap again to stop. This replaces the [SCAN]/[STOP] text label.
  2. The sweep is visible. The needle leaves a short trail on the dial and the LCD counts up 0.1 MHz every 60 ms, the same rate startScan already uses.
  3. The lock threshold is on the meter. A tangerine tick on the SIG row marks the 0.68 lock level, so you can see the scan is about to stop before it does.P0
  4. Unplugged is a state, not a toast. A coral NO ANT badge, a dimmed frequency, a grey parked needle, and a plain line explaining that the headphone cable is the antenna. The ON AIR lamp goes dark.P0
  5. Only dead controls dim. The tuning wheel, MODE, presets and SCAN dim. BAND stays live so NET is one slide away.
  6. Behaviour decision needed. The code today flips to NET by itself on unplug. This mockup stays on FM and explains why, and the user chooses NET. Either way works; the state just needs a screen.
