For Modern Phones & Players
Spindle Standard
Best for phones and music players running Android 8 or newer (made in the last 6–8 years). Features animated spinning reels, lyrics synced to the beat, FM radio, and a 10-band tone equalizer.

Spindle Lite

How to Install in 3 Steps

Getting Spindle onto your device takes less than a minute.

1
Grab the APK
Tap either Spindle Standard or Spindle Lite above to download the APK directly to your phone.
2
Tap to Install
Open your Downloads folder and tap the file. If asked, allow your browser or file manager permission to install.
3
Press Play & Enjoy
Open Spindle, grant storage access to scan your songs, and watch the tape roll!
💡 Dedicated Walkman Tip: Want the ultimate retro experience? Set Spindle as your default home screen in your Android Settings (under Default Apps → Home app). Every time you turn on your device or press the Home button, boom—straight to your music player!
