## What's Changed

### 🏷️ No third-party product names
- The deck style options are now **FACEPLATE · SILVER '81** and **FACEPLATE · PRO BLACK**.
- The hold switch setting is now **HOLD Switch Lock**, and its toast reads "HOLD: ON / OFF".
- Two cassette shells were renamed: **Red Normal Type I** and **Ferrichrome Type III**. Their looks are unchanged.
- Your saved deck style, hold switch setting and selected cassette shell are migrated automatically on first launch.

### 🛠️ Fixes
- Screens no longer stay shrunk and dimmed after returning from the lock screen or switching pages.
